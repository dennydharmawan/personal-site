# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and engineering leaders evaluating Denny Dharmawan for work opportunities. They arrive with limited time, usually from a resume, profile link, or direct referral, and need a fast read on engineering scope, judgment, domain fit, and how to make contact.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Denny as a full-stack engineer for fintech, internal platforms, and operational systems work. Success means visitors understand the kind of systems he builds, trust the quality of his engineering judgment, and have a clear path to contact him or review his resume.

## Brand Personality

Precise, reliable, product-minded.

The voice should feel calm and specific: confident without overselling, technical without becoming insular, and polished without looking like a generic template.

## Anti-references

This should not feel like a generic AI SaaS landing page, a loud self-promotional portfolio, or a gimmicky animation showcase. Avoid repeating seniority claims everywhere, over-explaining the work with recruiter-signal labels, and using decorative effects that distract from engineering credibility.

## Design Principles

1. Show engineering judgment through concrete work, not inflated claims.
2. Keep the page recruiter-readable: fast hierarchy, clear roles, direct contact paths.
3. Let motion signal craft, but never compete with the content.
4. Use restraint as confidence: enough visual distinction to be memorable, not so much that it feels performative.
5. Keep copy specific to fintech, internal tools, access control, reliability, and product delivery.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and interactive controls. Maintain reduced-motion support for entrance and hover animations. Keep navigation usable without URL hash changes, preserve semantic links for external destinations and downloads, and avoid color-only meaning.
